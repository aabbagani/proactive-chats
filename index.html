Maple Town: Proactive Chats Concept

Independent case study, not an official Granicus product. Maple Town is a made-up town, and every service, step, date and contact here is made up. Nothing is sent.

A government AI agent answers a question, then shows the next steps people usually need, each with a reason. It tracks a deadline only if the resident taps Remind me.

    Proactive Chats adds one thing to a government AI agent: after an answer, offer the next step, and track it only if the resident agrees.

    Rules it follows

    • Steps come only from the agency's list. The agent never makes one up.
    • Every step shows why it's suggested, and the whole list stays visible.
    • No reminder without a tap. Stop ends all reminders.
    • One reminder per step. Same-day reminders arrive as one message.
    • Agencies can switch suggestions off for sensitive services.

    Left out of this prototype

    • Real messages, logins and saved data. Everything resets on reload.
    • The agency's own systems confirming a step is done.
    • Other languages, and steps that belong to other agencies.
    • Phone and in-person service.
    • Time passing. "Skip to reminder day" stands in for it.
