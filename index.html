Proactive Chats
A concept for Granicus' GXA AI agent

Granicus, a GovTech company, created GXA, an AI agent that answers residents' questions for local governments. My idea is to take that one step further. With Proactive Chats, after an answer the agent shows the next steps people usually need and tracks a deadline only if the resident taps Remind me.

To try it, we're simulating a made-up town called Maple Town, whose residents are using Proactive Chats.
Pick a question in the chat, or press Play demo, and let's see how it goes.

    Rules it follows

    • Steps come only from the agency's list, never made up.
    • Every step shows why it's suggested, and the whole list stays visible.
    • No reminder without a tap. Stop ends all reminders.
    • One reminder per step. Same-day reminders arrive as one message.
    • Agencies can switch suggestions off for sensitive services.

    Left out of this prototype

    • Real messages, logins and saved data. Everything resets on reload.
    • The agency's own systems confirming a step is done.
    • Other languages, and steps that belong to other agencies.
    • Phone and in-person service.
    • Time passing. "Jump ahead to the reminder" stands in for it.
